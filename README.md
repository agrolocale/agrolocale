# 🌱 Agrolocale

### Building Sustainable Communities Through Agriculture, Hospitality, Food, Real Estate, and Technology.

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Agrolocale**.

Agrolocale is building an ecosystem of innovative businesses and digital solutions focused on agriculture, sustainable living, hospitality, food, and technology.

This organization serves as the central home for the software, digital infrastructure, internal tools, and technology powering **Agrolocale and its subsidiaries**.

---

## 🌍 Our Ecosystem

### 🌱 Agrolocale

The parent brand focused on creating opportunities through agriculture, land, real estate, and sustainable development.

🌐 https://www.agrolocale.com

---

### 🌿 Elysian Farm and Resort

**Elysian Farm and Resort** is focused on creating unique experiences that combine nature, agriculture, relaxation, hospitality, and sustainable living.

Its technology ecosystem may include digital platforms for reservations, customer experiences, property management, operations, and more.

---

### 🍽️ Foodlocale

**Foodlocale** is focused on connecting people with food, agricultural produce, and innovative food solutions.

Its digital ecosystem may include platforms and tools supporting food discovery, ordering, agricultural products, logistics, and customer experiences.
